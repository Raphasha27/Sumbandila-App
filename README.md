# 🇿🇦 Sumbandila-app: Official Institutional Verification Portal

### 🔒 OFFICIAL GOVERNMENT USE ONLY - STRICT COMPLIANCE REQUIRED

The Sumbandila app is a specialized, secure platform designed for the **verification of professional and institutional credentials** across the Republic of South Africa. This application is intended for official use and operates under strict legal frameworks to ensure public safety and institutional integrity.

---

## 🏛️ Legal Compliance & Acts
This application is developed to comply with the following South African legislative frameworks:

1.  **POPIA (Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013)**: All data processing within Sumbandila adheres to strict privacy standards. No personal data is stored beyond the session requirement, and institutional registry data is accessed via official, secure government channels.
2.  **The Higher Education Act (Act 101 of 1997)**: Ensures that all educational institutions verified through this portal are recognized by the Department of Higher Education and Training (DHET).
3.  **The Health Professions Act (Act 56 of 1974)**: Governs the verification of medical professionals through the Health Professions Council of South Africa (HPCSA).
4.  **The Legal Practice Act (Act 28 of 2014)**: Governs the verification of legal practitioners through the Legal Practice Council (LPC).

---

## 🛡️ Security Architecture & Protection
Sumbandila employs multi-layered security to protect citizens from fraud and online scams:

*   **Verified Official Registry Mapping**: Each institution is visually mapped to its official, registered physical location to prevent "ghost schools" or scam clinics.
*   **Anti-Scam Redirects**: Users are provided with verified secure portals for institutions, preventing redirection to phishing or cloned websites.
*   **Real-time Fraud Alerts**: The dashboard includes live alerts for known unaccredited institutions and common scam patterns currently active in the region.
*   **Encrypted Data Transmission**: All verification requests are encrypted to ensure the integrity of the information provided by official registries.

---

## ⚠️ STRICT WARNING: FRAUDULENT ACTIVITY
**It is a criminal offense** to misrepresent credentials or operate an unaccredited institution under South African law. Any user who identifies a suspicious or unaccredited institution through this portal should report it immediately to the relevant regulatory body (DOH, DHET, or LPC).
