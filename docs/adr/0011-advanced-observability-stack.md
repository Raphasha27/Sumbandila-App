# ADR 0011: Advanced Observability Dashboarding (Grafana)

## Status
Accepted

## Context
While OpenTelemetry and Jaeger provide "Distributed Tracing" (the 'What' and 'Where' of a request), we lack a "Bird's Eye View" of the platform's aggregate health (the 'How well').

## Decision
We will deploy **Prometheus** for metric collection and **Grafana** for real-time visualization.

### Visualization Strategy:
1. **AI Risk Pulse**: Real-time graph of risk scores generated by the AI Sentinel.
2. **Registry Throughput**: Counter of certificates issued vs. revoked per minute.
3. **Queue Health**: Gauge for Redis Stream depth to identify consumer lag.
4. **Service Health**: CPU/RAM utilization across all 4 microservices.

## Consequences
- **Pros**: Scientific performance tuning, proactive capacity planning, professional "NOC" (Network Operations Center) capability.
- **Cons**: Requires additional container resources for the monitoring stack.
